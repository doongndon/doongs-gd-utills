# Daily Challenge

A roguelike run on a new level every day, the same for everyone, with a shared leaderboard.
Open it from the round card button at the bottom of the main menu. A red dot on the button
means you have not cleared anything yet today.

## Pick a difficulty

Each day has one level for every tab, and every tab is played as a roguelike run:

- **Main** - one of the main levels
- **Easy, Normal, Hard, Harder, Insane, Demon** - a random rated level of that difficulty
  (Demon means Easy Demon), drawn from the Geometry Dash servers

Every difficulty has its own cards and its own leaderboard. The level card shows the
difficulty, stars, creator and length, your best run and your rank. Press play to go
straight to the level.

## The run

At 25%, 50% and 75% the game stops and deals three cards face down. They flip over, you pick
one, it flies into your card slots at the top left, and the run carries on after 3, 2, 1.
On a computer you can also pick with the 1, 2 and 3 keys, or the arrow keys and Enter.

- **Help cards** cost points: Shield survives one death, Slow runs the game at 0.85x.
- **Curses** earn points: Mirror, Mini, Rush, Dark, Quake, Ghost, Silence, Blackout.

Dying loses every card - the next attempt starts empty. A clear scores 100 plus your cards;
the highest score wins and fewer attempts breaks ties. Everyone playing the same difficulty
gets the same cards that day. You can look at today's cards in the Cards tab before you play.

Practice mode and start positions do not count. Attempts count from the first time you open the
level that day, practice runs included.

## Language

The window, the cards, the picker and the HUD are in Korean by default. Switch to English
under Language in the mod settings. Korean text is drawn in a bundled bitmap font made
from BM JUA (Woowa Brothers, SIL Open Font License 1.1), so no other mod is needed.

## Fair play

Results are taken on trust. The server cannot see how a level was beaten - this is a game,
not a tournament.
