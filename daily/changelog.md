# v2.0.2
 - Fixed the featured border: GD's border sprite sits above the face's centre, so it peeked out over the top of the face. Featured levels now get a gold ring drawn around the face, and epic, legendary and mythic levels a flame ring in orange, blue or purple

# v2.0.1
 - Fixed online difficulties stuck on "the server is still picking this level": when the server cannot reach the GD servers, your game searches the GD servers itself, picks the level the same way and tells the server, which keeps the first pick for everyone
 - The level card shows when a level is being picked, and why picking failed; refresh tries again

# v2.0.0
 - Korean by default: every text in the mod, the card names and descriptions included, with a bundled Korean font (English in the settings)
 - New look: hand-drawn cards for every help card and curse, a card back, and a new menu button
 - The card picker deals the cards face down, flips them, and sends your pick flying into its slot
 - 3, 2, 1, GO! countdown with its own sounds, plus sounds for dealing, flipping, picking and shields (can be turned off)
 - Pick with the 1, 2, 3 keys, or the arrow keys and Enter, on a computer
 - New run HUD: difficulty, a score that counts up, and three card slots; markers on the progress bar show where the cards come
 - Shield breaks flash the screen and make your icon blink while you are safe
 - Redesigned Daily Challenge window: difficulty faces as tabs, a level card with stars, creator and length, a big play button
 - The ranking shows the cards each player used, highlights your row, and keeps you on the list even outside the top 10
 - New Cards tab shows today's cards for each stop; tap one to see it full size
 - Live countdown to the next challenge, a help page, and a red dot on the menu button until you clear something today
 - Saved online levels open straight on their level page

# v1.0.0
 - The Daily Challenge, moved out of Doong's GD Utils into a mod of its own
 - Every day is a roguelike run: pick Main, Easy, Normal, Hard, Harder, Insane or Easy Demon and play that level with cards at 25, 50 and 75%
 - Each difficulty has its own cards and its own leaderboard
 - Uses the official server, nothing to set up
